## Issue identified

Section 9 reserves disqualification for serious, deliberate, and sustained violations
"and only after a full hearing by an independent court, not by an administrative body".
Sections 7.6 and 8.10 both say the order must be made by "the High Court". The two
formulations are not the same. "An independent court" could include the District Court or
a specialist tribunal; it leaves the forum uncertain at the point where the consequence is
most severe. Disqualification removes a person from the electoral process and engages s 12
NZBORA (the right to stand for election). The proposal should name the forum, as it does
elsewhere, rather than leave it to inference.

This is a consistency defect, not a policy disagreement. The threshold ("serious,
deliberate, and sustained") and the requirement of a full hearing are unchanged.

## Proposed change

In s 9, replace "an independent court" with "the High Court":

> - **Disqualification:** reserved only for serious, deliberate, and sustained violations,
>   and only after a full hearing by the High Court, not by an administrative body.

No other provision changes. No clause is renumbered.

## Reasoning

Naming the High Court does three things:

- it makes s 9 consistent with ss 7.6 and 8.10, so the same forum makes every
  disqualification order under the proposal;
- it removes any argument that a lower court or tribunal could disqualify a candidate;
  and
- it preserves the full-hearing requirement and the seriousness threshold, so the change
  does not broaden the power to disqualify.

Disqualification is reserved for the most serious category of misconduct and is the
proposal's strongest sanction short of prosecution. A uniform High Court forum is
proportionate to that consequence and consistent with the s 5 analysis in s 10: the limit
on s 12 electoral rights is confined to a judicial process with a full hearing.

## Risks and counter-arguments

*The High Court is slow and expensive, and a small party may be unable to participate.*
The provision concerns disqualification of a candidate or party, not access to the
Commission's ordinary complaints process; ss 7 and 8 keep their own enforcement routes.
The serious, deliberate, and sustained threshold means the case will already be
substantial. A uniform High Court forum is the better guarantee of a full hearing.

*"Independent court" was deliberately flexible.* If so, the flexibility is not exercised
anywhere else in the proposal, and the ambiguity is a drafting cost without a demonstrated
benefit. If a different forum is intended, it should be named.

None. This is a consistency fix, not a resolution of Q1–Q8.
